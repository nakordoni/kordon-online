# Kordon Online: Border Queues

### Know the queue before you reach the border.

Kordon Online shows live queues and wait times at Ukraine's border crossings, so you can choose the best crossing and plan your trip with confidence. Clear, fast and made for real journeys.

## Features

- **Live queues and wait times** at Ukraine's border crossings
- **Wait-time forecast** to help you pick the right moment to go
- **Crossing reviews summary** from fellow travellers at a glance
- **Map with filters and points of interest** along the way
- **Bus ranking** to compare services
- **Notifications** so you do not have to keep checking
- **Apple Watch app** for the queue on your wrist
- **Dark theme** for comfortable night-time use
